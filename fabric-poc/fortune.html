What works and what is still a prototype

Native FortuneSheet formatting, selection statistics, sizing, freezing and copy. App integration protects values, sorts whole records, validates form changes, and saves records and presentation to this browser. Formatting follows record IDs after sorting.

Export CSV contains data only. Print/PDF uses FortuneSheet’s native formatted HTML range export in a flat report. Use your browser’s Save as PDF. Colours depend on your print background setting.

No server authentication, cross-device saving or production permissions yet. Browser storage can be cleared; it is not a business-data backup. Native selection statistics may treat text starting with numbers as numeric and suppress zero summaries in this library version.
